without permission. Attribution is not required by the licence. It is given here

Nothing else is changed.

Chosen because it is a picture of lit shapes against black. Pixelation keeps
whatever survives being averaged into a block, so a frame with hard bright
shapes in it still reads at eight pixels across while a frame of fine texture
turns to mud. Signage at night is almost the ideal case: the shapes are big,
the contrast is extreme, and you can still tell what it is long after the
detail has gone.
