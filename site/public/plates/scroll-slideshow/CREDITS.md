They were chosen for the geometry rather than the subject. This effect warps the
edges of the frame, and a bending straight line is the only thing that makes a
warp legible: a soft or empty picture hides its own distortion. All four carry
regular structure right out to the edge.

These are demonstration images for this repository. The prompt does not copy them
into your project, and it does not reference them. Use your own.
