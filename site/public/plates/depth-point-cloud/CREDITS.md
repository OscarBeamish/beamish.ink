The same plate AsciiImage uses, and chosen again for a different reason. This
item reads brightness as height, so it wants a picture whose tones already
describe its space: a lit headland in front, fog lying pale along the middle
distance, dark water behind. A flatly lit picture comes apart into a sheet with
texture on it, which proves nothing about depth.
