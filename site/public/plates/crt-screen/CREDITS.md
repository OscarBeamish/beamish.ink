# Demo photograph

One photograph from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), used under the [Unsplash
License](https://unsplash.com/license), which permits free commercial use
without permission. Attribution is not required by the licence. It is given here
because the work is someone else's.

It is cropped to 4:3 from the lower half of the frame, and recompressed.
Nothing else is changed.

Chosen for what a tube does to it, and cropped to 4:3 because that is the shape
of one. The signage gives saturated colour and hard white type, which is where
the bloom and the convergence error show; the crowd and the shaded buildings
give deep shadow, which is where the vignette, the rolling bar and the lift the
grille leaves in the blacks are visible at all. A daylight photograph with an
even tone across it hides every one of them.

The original frame is taller, and most of the top of it is empty night sky. That
version was tried first and the tube came out three-quarters black: correct, and
a poor advertisement for anything.
