The same two already used by AsciiImage and HalftoneMagnifier, chosen here for
how little they have in common. A tear between two pictures only reads as a
tear if the two sides disagree: same subject, same palette and the seam is
invisible however hard it is torn.

One is cool and foggy with a high horizon, the other is warm and green with the
sun in it. The horizons sit at different heights, which is what makes the shear
across the seam visible.
