The same photograph AsciiImage uses, and for a related reason. Error diffusion
is about tone, so the picture has to have some: a long soft gradient in the sky
is where the grain organises itself into something that follows the subject, and
it is also where every mistake shows. The diagonal streaking that `serpentine`
exists to cancel is invisible on foliage and unmissable on this sky, and so is
the shadow crush you get from dithering in gamma-encoded light.

A picture of texture alone would hide both faults and prove nothing.
