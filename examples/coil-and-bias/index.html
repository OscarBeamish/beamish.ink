Built to be opened

Every one of ours comes with the board layout, the bias procedure and a spare set of valves. An amplifier you cannot service is a rental you have paid for outright.

The range

Three chassis, one power supply design between them. The small one is not a cut down version of the large one, it is the same circuit with fewer output valves.
