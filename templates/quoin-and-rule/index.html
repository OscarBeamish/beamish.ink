Two pulls, the second once the first is dry

The register is the whole job. A sheet that has been through twice has to land within a hair of where it was, which is why the marks in the margin are printed before anything else and trimmed off last.

Paper with a tooth to it

Mould made, 300gsm, dampened before it goes on. Anything lighter takes the ink and none of the impression, and the impression is what you are paying for.

A press leaves a dent in the paper. That is not a flaw in the process, it is the process.
