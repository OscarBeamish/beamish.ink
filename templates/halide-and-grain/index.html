How a commission runs

Three stages. The middle one is the long one and we will not pretend otherwise.

  1. You tell us the place and the month

    Not the shot. The shot is the part that depends on weather we do not control, and a brief that specifies it is a brief that will be missed.

  2. We wait

    This is most of the schedule and all of the cost. The Skye commission took nine days, of which one produced anything, and the client was told to expect exactly that.

  3. You get the sheet, then the print

    Every frame from the trip, marked where we would print, and the final at whatever size the job needs.
